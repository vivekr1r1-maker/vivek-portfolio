## ✏️ How to Customize Personal Placeholders

1. **Profile Photo:**
   - Save your photograph as `vivek.jpg` inside the `assets/images/` directory.
   - In `index.html` (line 144) and `about.html` (line 74), change `assets/images/profile-placeholder.svg` to `assets/images/vivek.jpg`.

2. **Email Address:**
   - Open `contact.html` and `assets/docs/resume.html`.
   - Replace `[Add your email address]` with your real email (e.g. `vivek@example.com`).

3. **GitHub Profile:**
   - In `contact.html` and `index.html`, replace `[Add your GitHub profile]` with your GitHub profile link (e.g. `https://github.com/your-username`).

4. **GTTC Industrial Visit Date & Photos:**
   - In `projects.html`, update the date placeholder `[Editable Date / Month, Year]` to the actual date you attended.
   - Save your visit photos in `assets/images/` and update the `gttc-photo-placeholder` elements.

5. **Future Projects & Certifications:**
   - As you complete projects or earn certificates in subsequent semesters, edit the placeholder cards in `projects.html`.
